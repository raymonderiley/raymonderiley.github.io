The Ransomware Recovery Order: What Enterprise Infrastructure Needs to Restore First

When an enterprise environment is compromised by modern ransomware, standard recovery playbooks often fail due to cascading dependency locks. Establishing a strict, staged restoration hierarchy—starting with secure Identity & Authentication providers up through core network infrastructure and monitored business applications—is the only way to ensure operational continuity without reintroducing threats.
