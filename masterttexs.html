Enterprise Disaster Recovery & Resilience Scenarios

Over my several years in IT, I've encountered a wide array of infrastructure failures, cloud outages, and along with a few cyber incidents. This repository is a culmination of those experiences, offering a diverse set of tabletop exercises designed to challenge and enhance your disaster recovery and resilience strategies. AI has been utilized to enhance the situation and create new situations, as well as to create the base tabletop exercises. The exercises are designed to be used in a variety of settings, from small team discussions to large-scale organizational drills. Each exercise is accompanied by a set of discussion questions and a facilitator's guide to help you get the most out of your tabletop sessions. A comprehensive library of rigorous, peer-reviewed tabletop simulation exercises spanning on-premises, single-cloud, hybrid, multi-cloud, AI/LLM failure modes, and cyber-recovery domains.
