Professional Summary & Bio

With over 25 years of enterprise IT experience—including a decade dedicated to disaster recovery, cyber-recovery, and business continuity. As someone who has built two successful disaster recovery programs, I specialize in fortifying organizational resilience against complex operational disruptions. My background spans the design and implementation of robust enterprise infrastructure across IAM - Active Directory, Microsoft Entra ID, Okta, VMware, Zerto, VEEAM, and virtualized environments such as containerization technology.

Leadership & Strategy

True technological resilience requires strategic vision, cross-functional alignment, and empowering high-performing engineering teams. I excel at mentoring multi-disciplinary teams, fostering collaborative cultures of continuous learning, and driving accountability during critical incident management. By establishing clear incident response playbooks and governance frameworks, I bridge the gap between executive stakeholders and technical operators.

Innovation & Engineering

I truly love exploring emerging technologies and integrating innovative solutions into legacy and modern cloud environments and helps drive my daily workflow. In an era where AI and advanced automation redefine infrastructure capability, I embrace complex technical challenges head-on. The true reward lies in the collaborative rush of resolving critical problems and allowing the business to get back to normal swiftly and securely.

Core Philosophy

I believe secure infrastructures rely on strict baselines, rigorous auditing, and automation. By minimizing manual repetition through custom scripts, organizations can focus on scaling forward rather than firefighting recurring issues.

I also believe in being prepared for the unexpected, and that includes having a well-defined disaster recovery and cyber-recovery strategy. My goal is to ensure organizations can withstand disruptions and continue to operate effectively or build resilient systems that can adapt to changing circumstances.
