4. Escalation Path

  • Detect > Security on-call > CISO (within min) > CMT Lead ( min) > CEO ( min) > Board chair / Audit Cmte ( hrs) > Insurer ( hrs per policy) > Counsel (immediately)

5. Communication Channels

  • Primary CMT channel: Out-of-band (if corporate systems compromised):
  • CMT bridge line: Status cadence: every hrs Offline contact list held by:
  • Leader verification (anti-impersonation): callback to known number + code phrase held by . No payment or credential change on voice/email instruction alone.
  • Order of notification: Board > Insurer/Counsel > Employees > Key customers > Regulators > Media > Partners/Investors (adjust to legal obligations)

6. First 48 Hours

  • 0 to 1 hr: confirm incident; declare severity; convene CMT; move to out-of-band channel; engage counsel (privilege); start decision log
  • 1 to 4 hrs: decide BCP activation and revenue shutdown; notify insurer; engage forensics; holding statement approved; employee notice
  • 4 to 12 hrs: key-customer outreach; board briefing; inventory all reporting deadlines; sanctions check if extortion
  • 12 to 24 hrs: ransom position confirmed; customer and media statements; recovery options and timeline presented
  • 24 to 48 hrs: regulatory and contractual notices drafted and approved; materiality assessment; recovery plan and reopening criteria approved

7. Pre-Approved External Support

  • Insurer hotline / policy #: Breach counsel: Forensics / IR:
  • Ransom negotiator: Crisis PR: Law enforcement contact:

8. Decision Principles

  • Decide with the information available; record assumptions; set a time to revisit. Safety and legal obligations first, then data, then revenue. One voice externally. Facts confirmed vs. under investigation are always labeled. Every decision logged with owner, time and rationale.
