4. Escalation Path

  • Detection > Data owner > CISO + Head of Internal Audit ( min) > CMT Lead ( min) > CEO ( min) > Audit Committee chair ( hrs) > Insurers (per policy) > Employment and breach counsel (immediately) > Law enforcement (by decision)

5. Communication Rules

  • Need-to-know list held by: Investigation discussed only through counsel to preserve privilege
  • Never name or prejudge an individual in any internal or external communication
  • Staff message owner: Affected individuals' help line owner:
  • Payment instruction verification: no change to bank details or payments on email or phone instruction alone; confirm via
  • Order: Audit Committee > insurers and counsel > affected clients > affected individuals > staff > regulators > media

6. First 48 Hours

  • 0 to 1 hr: confirm deliberate alteration; declare; convene CMT; engage counsel; preserve logs and backups; start decision log
  • 1 to 4 hrs: decide processing approach; freeze non-essential changes; restrict access as decided; notify insurers
  • 4 to 12 hrs: name investigation lead; scope affected records and funds; client notification; Audit Committee briefed
  • 12 to 24 hrs: employee contact decision; law enforcement decision; staff message; make-whole decision
  • 24 to 48 hrs: recovery options presented; regulatory filing obligations inventoried; controls report impact assessed

7. Pre-Approved External Support

  • Forensic accountants: Employment counsel: Breach counsel:
  • Crime insurer / policy #: Cyber insurer / policy #: Independent data validation:

8. Decision Principles

  • Presume innocence; protect evidence; limit access. Preserve first, then correct, within an agreed time limit. People affected get help before blame is settled. No single person's word certifies data; certification is independent. Log every decision with owner, time and rationale.
