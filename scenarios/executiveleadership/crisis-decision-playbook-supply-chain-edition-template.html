4. Escalation Path

  • Customer report or vendor notice > Security on-call > CISO + Product owner ( min) > CMT Lead ( min) > CEO ( min) > Board chair ( hrs) > Insurer (per policy) > Counsel (immediately) > Vendor executive contact ( hrs)

5. Customer Communication and Verification

  • Single official channel: status / trust page at — every advisory is posted here first
  • Verification rule told to every customer: we never send tools or links by email; confirm any request via
  • Leader impersonation: callback to known number + code phrase held by . Public denial of fake media approved by
  • Notification order: safety-critical customers > regulated customers > all affected customers > employees > media > partners/investors

6. First 48 Hours

  • 0 to 1 hr: confirm our product is implicated; declare; convene CMT; engage counsel; freeze releases; start decision log
  • 1 to 4 hrs: kill switch decision; first customer advisory (known / unknown / next update); call safety-critical customers personally; notify insurer
  • 4 to 12 hrs: vendor contact; customer verification rule published; board briefed; inventory customers' regulatory deadlines
  • 12 to 24 hrs: extortion position; spokesperson statement; customer triage list; trading blackout decision
  • 24 to 48 hrs: recovery path options presented; customer data-impact facts shared; attestation owner named; materiality assessment begun

7. Pre-Approved External Support

  • Breach counsel: Software supply chain forensics: Independent code reviewer:
  • Insurer hotline / policy #: Negotiator: Crisis PR: Customer IR retainer:

8. Decision Principles

  • Customer safety before company liability. Tell customers what we know, what we don't and when we'll update them. Protect exposed customers regardless of any extortion decision. One voice to customers, one to the vendor, one to the public. Nobody attests to what they have not seen evidence for. Log every decision with owner, time and rationale.
