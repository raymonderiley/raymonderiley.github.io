5. Communication Channels

  • Customer status page: Update every min even when nothing has changed
  • Provider verification: confirm any provider request via known account manager at ; never pay through emailed links
  • Out-of-band CMT channel (not hosted on the affected provider):
  • Order: customers (status page) > employees > regulators > media > partners and investors

6. First 48 Hours

  • 0 to 1 hr: confirm provider outage; declare; convene CMT; status page live; start decision log; start insurance waiting-period clock
  • 1 to 4 hrs: failover decision against impact tolerance; data loss accepted by named owner; change freeze; shift rota
  • 4 to 12 hrs: restoration priority applied; emergency spend decisions; customer updates on a fixed cadence; board briefed
  • 12 to 24 hrs: staff rest enforced; paid priority and credit positions confirmed; regulatory reporting deadlines tracked
  • 24 to 48 hrs: failback criteria agreed; regulatory reports filed; reconciliation of lost transactions planned

7. Pre-Approved External Support

8. Decision Principles

  • Decide against agreed tolerances, not the provider's optimism. Set a time to decide, and decide at that time. Prioritize by written criteria, not by who calls loudest. Rested people make fewer recovery errors. Concentration risk is a board decision, not an IT default. Log every decision with owner, time and rationale.
